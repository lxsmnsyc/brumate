The element types have more HTML attributes: `meter form`, `dialog closedby`, the `shadowroot*` attributes of `template`, `style type`, the microdata attributes (`itemscope`, `itemtype`, `itemprop`, `itemid`, `itemref`) and `exportparts` on every element, and `xlink:href` and `xml:lang` on SVG elements. Some values now complete their known names and still accept any other string: `link as`, `script type`, `meta http-equiv`, the `iframe sandbox` tokens, the `autocomplete` tokens of `input`, `textarea` and `select`, the `button command` values, and `blocking`.
