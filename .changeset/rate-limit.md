'brumate': minor
---

`rateLimit({ max, window, key? })` from `brumate/server` limits how often a client can call a server function. Called first thing in the function, it counts the calls of each key in a sliding window and rejects the ones over the limit with a `RateLimitError`: status 429, `Retry-After`, and `retryAfter` in the browser. The key is the app's, such as the email of a sign-in or the ID of a user, from the event and the arguments, or else the address of the client. Each call counts with one atomic increment before the limit decides, so calls at the same time cannot all pass. `rateLimit.middleware(options)` limits every request under a folder of routes, or of the app from the entry middleware, and counts each route by its pattern, which `event.route` gives. `rateLimit.strict` is a preset for sign-ins. The counts live in memory, or in a store with an atomic increment, such as Redis, through `configureRateLimit({ store })`. `clientAddress(event)` reads the address of the client from the server, which passes it to `handle` as `address`, or from `X-Forwarded-For` when that address is one of the proxies that `configureRateLimit({ proxies })` names. A malformed range throws. Without a key or an address, a limit throws in production and warns in development. Limits count unless turned off, and never while the build prerenders.
