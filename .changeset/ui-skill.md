A skill for the parts, `skills/brumate-ui`, ships in the package. It has a `SKILL.md` and references for composition, slots and contexts, state with `$bind`, `as` and `ref`, templates, `data-*` styling, collections, overlays, dates and colors, and i18n. To use it, copy it into the skills of a project: `cp -r node_modules/@brumate/ui/skills/brumate-ui .claude/skills/`.
