A skill for the parts, `skills/brumate-ui`, ships in the package. It has a `SKILL.md` and references for composition, slots and contexts, state with `$bind`, `as` and `ref`, templates, `data-*` styling, collections, overlays, dates and colors, and i18n. The `brm` blocks of the skill are compiled and rendered in a test.
