ListBox, ListBoxItem, Menu, MenuItem, GridList, GridListItem, Tree, TreeItem, TabList, Tab, TabPanel, TagList and Tag take `as`, an element name or a component, with the props of the target, and forward `ref`. Their props types are `<Name>OwnProps` and `<Name>Props<T>`, and no longer take any attribute.
