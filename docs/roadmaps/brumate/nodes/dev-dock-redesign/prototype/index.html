Dev dock: prototype v2

  1. Closed, the dock is the grip on the bottom edge again. Hover or focus grows it into a tab with the mark, the version and the issues; it is red with errors.
  2. Open, the dock is attached to the bottom or right edge, full width or height. Drag its edge to resize. The page scrolls past it.
  3. The version of brumate and Vite stays in the header.
  4. Selected rows are filled with a soft ring, no coloured left edges.
  5. Splits with hairlines in place of cards in cards. A status line shows the route and live numbers.
  6. Resizable panels: drag the line between two columns, or focus it and use the arrow keys (Shift for bigger steps). Double-click resets. Sizes are kept for the next open.
  7. Graph is back, in Inspect: Values | Graph. Every signal, prop, memo, markup read and effect of a component, with its value. A write marks its node and every node after it in yellow. Click a node for what it reads and what reads it. Drag to pan, scroll or the buttons to zoom.
  8. 4 workspaces, ⌘K, server call details as in v1.

Try: hover the grip, Alt+Shift+D, ⌘K, Add, Pick, a call in Activity.
