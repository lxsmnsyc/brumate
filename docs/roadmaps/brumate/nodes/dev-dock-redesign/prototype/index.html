Dev dock: prototype

  1. Island in place of the grip: errors and live activity are visible while the dock is closed.
  2. 4 workspaces in place of 13 tabs: Inspect, Activity, Issues, Page.
  3. Inspect merges Components, Elements and Graph: tree, slots, and what reads each value.
  4. Activity merges Log, Timeline, Effects, Navigation and Server into one stream with lanes.
  5. Server calls: click a call in Activity for its arguments, result or error, timing and what ran on the server.
  6. ⌘K finds any component, value, call or action.
  7. The dock floats with a margin, and can sit at the bottom or on the right.

Try: click the island, Alt+Shift+D, ⌘K, the Add button, Pick.
