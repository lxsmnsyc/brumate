- Direction. Neither mechanism replaces the other without breaking tests:
  - `wait` cannot take the held writes: it splits only the instance script, while `fillSettled` works in block scripts and rows ("fills its text in a block script"). It holds the whole rest, while `fillSettled` holds only the writes that read the memo ("fills a memo that settles while another memo of the scope still loads"). And `wait` runs `setSlots` on the scope, which held writes must not do.
  - `fillSettled` cannot take `wait`: `wait` needs the latest-load rule on slot writes (a prop that changes during the wait), the range comments, the slots handed to the rest and `settledSlots`/`renderedLoads` for the refresh after the insert.
  - So `wait` and its boundary stay, and `fillSettled` is rewritten onto that boundary: it lives in waits.ts and counts in the boundary with `join`, `leave` and `fault`, as the contract says any kind of wait does. The copy in templates.ts and its imports of `fail` and `isThenable` are gone. The codegen no longer emits `fillSettled` for an awaited memo of the component in the render module.
