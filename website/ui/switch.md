# Switch

A native checkbox with `role="switch"`, for a setting that applies at once.

```brm demo SwitchDemo

```

- `isSelected` is its state. See [State](./state.md).
- It is a `<label>` around the input. Draw the switch in the children. See [Styling](./styling.md#boxes).
- `isDisabled` and `isReadOnly` set its state. `name` and `value` post with the form.
- The label shows `data-selected`, `data-pressed`, `data-hovered`, `data-focused`, `data-focus-visible`, `data-disabled` and `data-readonly`.
